# Portfolio logo sources

Logos identify the organizations associated with Laila’s experience and credentials. Downloaded assets are served locally; portfolio visitors do not depend on third-party logo services.

- **Google and Pendo:** transparent PNG renderings cleaned from the image files supplied by Laila in chat, with the image tool. Original uploads remain unchanged.
- **Opendoor:** https://github.com/asadkhalid305/opendoor-clone/blob/c3ded2d157da871acca51b1b9b15cb2e6f86b036/res/logo-opendoor.svg — blue company wordmark.
- **Western University:** https://github.com/zhoudaxia233/UniLogo/blob/d64657c0f29817d8beaaf541104446c54d39c383/images/imgs0617/Western%20University.png — university crest.
- **IO Industries:** https://github.com/nelsonong/ioi-calculators/blob/e8278c18e976e11b52c56e6f02483f7eee8b86a1/public/ioi-icon.png — black/red mark on a transparent background.
- **Hydro One:** https://github.com/WISE-UofT/wise-conference/blob/dcdcff0c2ba8f58eac905524237300b140849013/img/sponsors/hydro.png — conference sponsor logo.
- **The Peninsula:** standalone transparent PNG rendering cleaned from the existing portfolio masthead reference with the image tool; original screenshot unchanged.
- **The Western Gazette, Western MSA, and Women in Technology Society:** standalone transparent PNG renderings cleaned from the existing portfolio references with the image tool. These are background-cleaned reproductions, not newly downloaded official vector masters. The Gazette mark uses dark ink for readability on cream paper.
- **Western University display:** an SVG silhouette excludes stray rectangular edge artifacts from the sourced crest while preserving its original lettering and insignia.
- **Electricity Basics:** Electricity Canada was confirmed as the issuer by Laila. A generic lightning icon represents the topic; it is not an Electricity Canada brand mark. No credential URL or issue date has been supplied.

Project screenshots remain in Projects. Employer logos replace organization-name placeholders in Experience, the Western crest appears in Education, and credential issuers appear in Certificates.

Article logos are centered. Google and Pendo display margins are tightened and their certificate logos use a larger, responsive frame. The cleaned logo PNGs have real alpha transparency and display without a blend-mode workaround or decorative backing boxes.
